Same corner.
Same griddle.

If you grew up around Denver Harbor, you already know. Dixie Maid has been feeding this neighborhood from the same corner of Lyons Ave for as long as most folks can remember — some regulars have been coming for thirty-plus years.

Nothing here gets made ahead. The patty hits the griddle when you order. The fries get cut from real potatoes. The shakes come thick enough to bend the straw, and the shrimp gets dropped fresh in the fryer.

John's usually at the counter, and odds are he'll come check that your food came out right. Order at the window, grab a booth, take your time.
